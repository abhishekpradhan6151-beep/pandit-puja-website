About the Puja

Mahamrityunjaya Jaap

Mahamrityunjaya Jaap is a traditional Vedic chanting practice centred around the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra. The ritual is performed according to Vedic procedures with appropriate chanting and puja vidhi.

The puja can be arranged as an online ritual or as an in-person service, subject to availability and location.
